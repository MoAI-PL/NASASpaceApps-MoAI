| | Real pre-flight draws (n = 4 crew, 12 draws) | Simulator (healthy) |
|---|---|---|
| Mean (mg/dL) | 75.3 (range 56–100) | 90 |
| Spread between people (sd) | 3.8 | 8 |

Real mean inside the simulated population (±2 sd): **yes** (difference -14.7 mg/dL). Within-person spread real / simulated: 2.1. Comparison only; the simulator was not calibrated on these data. n = 4 crew, 3 pre-flight draws each; fasting state of the draws is not stated in the table.
